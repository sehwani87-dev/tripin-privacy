Tripin

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Tripin is an iOS app that helps you record what you spend while traveling and review it on a map. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices available to you.

Your complete trip and expense records are stored on your device. Tripin does not offer accounts or sync to a server of its own. When you use the map or place search, the map region or search request you make may be sent to an external service.

1. Operator and scope

This policy applies to the Tripin iOS app, provided by an individual developer, and to this privacy policy page. Tripin does not operate accounts or a backend server of its own. Questions about the handling of personal information can be sent to the email address at the bottom of this policy.

2. Information handled and purposes

Trip and expense records

Trip name, city, dates, budget, currency, place of expense, category, amount, date, address, and the location coordinates saved with an expense are used for trip management, expense records, and budget and statistics displays. These records are stored on your device and are not transmitted to the developer's server.

Location information

When you grant location permission and use the current-location feature or place search, your current location is used to move the map and to search for nearby places. The app does not track your location in the background, and does not continuously store your device's current location on the developer's server.

Advertising and related information

The app may use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage the related consent. For the purposes of serving ads, frequency capping, preventing abuse, measuring performance, and managing consent, Google may process information such as approximate location inferred from your IP address, device identifiers (for example an advertising identifier or an app- or developer-scoped identifier), ad impression information, product interactions such as app launches and taps, and crash logs and performance or diagnostic information.

The Google User Messaging Platform may communicate with Google before the ATT decision in order to determine whether a region-specific consent flow is required and to present the necessary consent screen. No ads are requested at this stage.

After completing this consent flow, and before starting the Google Mobile Ads SDK or requesting any ad, the app checks the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization status. If you allow tracking through ATT, your device's advertising identifier and your activity in other companies' apps and websites may be used for personalized advertising or ad performance measurement, subject as well to the advertising consent choices that apply in your region. If you do not allow tracking through ATT, or tracking is restricted, the app disables ad personalization and the publisher first-party ID feature. Even in that case, Google may process your IP address, device signals, or certain identifiers in order to serve non-personalized or limited ads, apply frequency capping, produce aggregate reporting, and prevent abuse. The core features of the app — trip management, the map, and expense records — remain available whether or not you allow ATT.

3. Information not collected

The app does not offer account registration and does not ask for your name, a password, payment card numbers, or bank account information. There is no inquiry form inside the app. If you contact the developer directly at the email address below, the email service may process your email address and the contents of your message.

4. External services and transfers

The following external services are used for app features and to serve ads.

  • Apple Maps (MapKit): used for map display and place search. When a map is displayed or moved, general network information such as the map region being viewed may be processed by Apple. When you run a place search, the search term and a search area centered on your current location are sent to Apple. If your current location is unavailable, the city name saved with the currently selected trip may be appended to the search term to improve accuracy. Search results may be held temporarily in app memory; search terms that you do not save as a trip record are not separately stored in the app's persistent storage. Apple Maps is a feature built into iOS, and the handling of map and search requests follows Apple's policy. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Frankfurter exchange rate API: used to convert expenses entered in another currency into your trip currency, and to power the exchange calculator. Each request sends only two currency codes and a reference date; expense amounts, trip details, and location are never sent. Past expenses use the rate for the date of the expense, while today's expenses and the calculator use the latest rate. Retrieved rates are held temporarily in app memory and are not written to the device's persistent storage. Frankfurter
  • Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform: used to serve ads and manage consent. Details about Google's handling of information are available in the Google Privacy Policy and in how Google handles information in apps that use Google services.
  • GitHub Pages: used to publicly host this privacy policy page. When you open this page, GitHub may process general access information such as your IP address, browser, and request logs in accordance with its own policy. GitHub Privacy Statement

The service providers above may process information on servers outside your country, including in the countries where they operate. The specific countries of processing, retention periods, deletion methods, and processing practices follow each provider's policy and your consent choices. The developer reviews each service's terms and privacy policy so that external service providers protect information at an appropriate level in accordance with the relevant agreements, applicable law, and Apple's requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

Trip and expense records are kept on your device until you delete them in the app. You can delete an individual expense, an entire trip, or every record stored on the device at once using Delete All Trip Data in Settings. Deleting the app generally also deletes the app data stored on your device. Whether data is retained through an iOS backup or your device settings depends on Apple's policy and your device configuration.

Retention and deletion of information processed by the place search service and the advertising services follow each provider's policy. Requests to review or delete information under the developer's control can be sent to the contact address below.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can change location permission at any time in the Tripin permission screen in iPhone Settings.
  • You can change whether Tripin is allowed to track under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone.
  • You can edit or delete trips and expense records directly in the app.
  • You can export the trips and expenses stored on your device as a CSV file using Export Data in Settings. The exported file goes to the location or app you choose and is not sent to the developer.
  • You can erase everything stored on the device at once using Delete All Trip Data in Settings.
  • Where it is offered in your region, you can review or change your advertising consent from the privacy choices menu in the app.
  • To access, correct, or delete information under the developer's control, or to ask about the handling of personal information, use the contact address below.

7. Protection of information

Trip and expense records are stored inside the iOS app sandbox. The app's network requests to external services use HTTPS. That said, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure, and the developer works to maintain reasonable protections appropriate to the app's features and the nature of the information handled.

8. Children's privacy

Tripin is not a service designed for children, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child's personal information has been processed, please let the developer know at the contact address below.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised if the app's features, the external services it uses, or applicable law change. Significant changes will be announced on this page or through the app, and the effective date will be updated.
